📖 Why Linked Lists Exist

Arrays have one major limitation.

Imagine this array:

[10] [20] [30] [40]

Insert 15 at index 1.

Everything after it must move.

[10] [15] [20] [30] [40]

That's O(n).

Instead...

A Linked List stores each element separately.

+------+      +------+      +------+
| 10 | •| ---> |20 | •| ---> |30 | X|
+------+      +------+      +------+

Each node knows only the address of the next node.

No shifting required.

❌ Common Mistakes
Losing the next node before changing pointers.
Forgetting to update the head.
Null pointer errors.
Infinite loops.
Returning the wrong head.
Mixing node values with node references.
