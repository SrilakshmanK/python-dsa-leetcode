Master Hash Maps and Sets to perform fast lookups, frequency counting, duplicate detection, and optimize brute-force solutions.

Outcome:

📖 Why Hash Maps Exist

Imagine you're solving this problem:

Find whether a duplicate exists in an array.

Brute Force:

Compare every element with every other element

↓

O(n²)

Can we do better?

Yes.

Store previously seen numbers.

Lookup becomes:

O(1)

Overall solution:

O(n)

That's why Hash Maps exist.
