📖 Why Binary Search Exists

Imagine finding a name in a phone book.

Would you start from page one?

No.

You open the middle.

Middle

↓

Too Small?

↓

Go Right

↓

Too Large?

↓

Go Left

Every comparison removes half of the remaining search space.

Instead of checking every element:

O(n)

You check:

O(log n)

That's why Binary Search exists.
