📖 Why Two Pointers Exist

Imagine you're asked:

Find two numbers that add up to a target.

Brute Force:

↓

O(n²)

If the array is sorted...

Can we move from both ends?

Left Pointer →

← Right Pointer

Now each element is visited only once.

O(n)

That's why the Two Pointer technique exists.
