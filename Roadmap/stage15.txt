⏱️ Complexity

Most Backtracking problems are exponential.

Typical complexities:

Problem	Complexity
Subsets	O(2ⁿ)
Permutations	O(n!)
N-Queens	Exponential
Sudoku	Exponential

Understand:

Backtracking is usually slow by nature.

The goal is correctness first.

Optimization comes later.
