📘 Phase 3 — Core Data Structures & Algorithms
Stage 9 — Recursion
🎯 Objective

Understand recursion as a problem-solving technique by learning how a function can solve a problem by calling itself and mastering the concept of the call stack.

Outcome:
After completing this stage, you should confidently identify recursive problems, write recursive solutions, trace recursive calls, and convert simple recursive solutions into iterative ones when appropriate.

📖 Why Recursion Exists

Imagine climbing stairs.

To reach stair 10, you first need to reach stair 9.

To reach stair 9, you first need to reach stair 8.

Eventually...

10

↓

9

↓

8

↓

...

↓

1

Every step depends on solving a smaller version of the same problem.

That's recursion.

Instead of solving one huge problem,

solve a smaller version,

until the problem becomes trivial.

🎯 Interview Tips

Whenever you solve a recursive problem, ask:

What is my base case?
What is my smaller problem?
What does my recursive function return?
What happens after the recursive call returns?

If you can't answer these,

don't code yet.
