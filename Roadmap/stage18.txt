❌ Common Mistakes
Choosing DP when Greedy is sufficient.
Using sorting when Prefix Sum is enough.
Missing interval overlap cases.
Incorrect Union Find implementation.
Overusing Bit Manipulation.
Trying to force one pattern onto every problem.
