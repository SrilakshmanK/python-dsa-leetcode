Stage 11 — Queue & Deque
🎯 Objective

Master the Queue and Deque data structures to efficiently solve problems involving sequential processing, breadth-first traversal, scheduling, and sliding window techniques.

Outcome:
After completing this stage, you should confidently recognize Queue-based problems, implement Queue and Deque operations, and solve common interview questions.

📖 Why Queues Exist

Imagine people standing in a ticket counter.

The first person who enters the line gets served first.

Front

↓

[A] [B] [C] [D]

              ↑

            Rear

Person A entered first.

Person A leaves first.

First In

↓

First Out

This is called FIFO (First In, First Out).

❌ Common Mistakes
Using a Python List as a Queue (pop(0) is O(n)).
Forgetting FIFO order.
Confusing Queue with Stack.
Removing from the wrong end of a Deque.
Forgetting empty queue checks.
