Stage 14 — Heap (Priority Queue)
🎯 Objective

Master Heaps and Priority Queues by understanding how they efficiently maintain the smallest or largest element and solve common interview problems involving priorities, rankings, and Top-K elements.

Outcome:
After completing this stage, you should confidently recognize Heap problems, use Python's heapq module, and solve Easy and Medium interview questions.

📖 Why Heaps Exist

Imagine a hospital emergency room.

Patients aren't treated in the order they arrive.

Instead:

Critical Patient

↓

Highest Priority

↓

Treated First

A normal Queue can't do this.

A Heap can.

A Heap always keeps the highest (or lowest) priority element ready.
