📖 Why Trees Exist

Imagine organizing files.

Would you store everything in one folder?

No.

You naturally create folders inside folders.

Documents
│
├── College
│   ├── MCA
│   └── Certificates
│
└── Personal
    ├── Photos
    └── Resume

That's a Tree.

Trees are used whenever data has a parent-child relationship.

Examples:

File Systems
HTML DOM
Organization Charts
Decision Trees
Databases
Compilers
