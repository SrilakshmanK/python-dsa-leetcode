Stage 3 — Arrays
🎯 Objective

Master arrays by understanding how data is stored, accessed, modified, and traversed while solving interview-style problems.

Outcome:
After completing this stage, you should be able to recognize array-based problems, implement efficient solutions, and comfortably solve Easy and Medium array questions on LeetCode.

📖 Why Arrays Exist

Arrays are the most fundamental data structure.

Almost every data structure (Stacks, Queues, Heaps, Hash Tables, etc.) is built using arrays internally.

Mastering arrays is the first step toward mastering DSA.

🎯 Interview Tips

When you see an array problem, ask:

Can I solve this with one traversal?
Do I need two traversals?
Can I avoid nested loops?
Can I use extra memory?
Can I solve it in-place?

Always think about optimization after getting the brute-force solution working.
