📖 Why Sliding Window Exists

Imagine this problem:

Find the maximum sum of any subarray of size k.

Brute Force:

Check every possible subarray

↓

O(n × k)

Instead...

Maintain a window.

[ Window ]

Move one step.

Remove one element.

Add one element.

O(n)

That's why Sliding Window exists.
