📖 Why Stacks Exist

Imagine a stack of plates.

You always place a new plate on the top.

You always remove the top plate.

      ┌─────┐
Top → │  5  │
      ├─────┤
      │  4  │
      ├─────┤
      │  3  │
      ├─────┤
      │  2  │
      ├─────┤
      │  1  │
      └─────┘

The last plate you put in is the first one you remove.

Last In

↓

First Out

That's called LIFO (Last In, First Out).
