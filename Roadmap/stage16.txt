Master Graphs by understanding graph representations, traversal algorithms, connected components, shortest paths, and common interview patterns.

Outcome:
After completing this stage, you should confidently recognize Graph problems and solve Easy and Medium graph interview questions using DFS and BFS.

📖 Why Graphs Exist

Imagine Google Maps.

Cities are connected by roads.

      A
     / \
    B---C
     \ /
      D

Questions become:

Can I reach D?
What is the shortest path?
Is there a cycle?
How many separate groups exist?

A Graph models relationships between connected objects.

❌ Common Mistakes
Forgetting the visited set.
Infinite traversal due to cycles.
Mixing DFS and BFS.
Using recursion without considering stack depth.
Traversing disconnected graphs incorrectly.
Forgetting graph direction (directed vs undirected).
